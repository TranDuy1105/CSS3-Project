Welcome

Manchester United Football Club is one of the most successful football clubs in the world. Founded in 1878, the club has won numerous domestic and international trophies.

This website introduces the club, some of its current players, and the major trophies won throughout its history.

Old Trafford

Old Trafford, often called the "Theatre of Dreams", has been Manchester United's home stadium since 1910 and can accommodate more than 74,000 supporters.
